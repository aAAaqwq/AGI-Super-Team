# TikTok and Baijiahao legacy workflows

These platforms exist only as historical/example paths in the audited project and are not current unified CLI capabilities. Treat all steps as unverified live-UI landmarks.

## TikTok Studio video

1. Open TikTok Studio and confirm the account.
2. Let the user complete login and any security challenge.
3. Open Upload and select one video.
4. Wait for the editor and a clearly enabled Post control.
5. Fill caption/title and hashtags.
6. Upload and confirm a cover when supplied.
7. Choose immediate or scheduled posting and verify timezone.
8. Review account, media, caption, hashtags, cover, disclosure, visibility, and schedule.
9. Click Post once.
10. Verify the newest item and content ID in TikTok Studio Content.

Do not copy the audited source's unbounded publish retry loop or automatic language switching. Stop when the live UI does not match.

## Baijiahao video

1. Open Baijiahao Creator Center and confirm the account.
2. Let the user complete login and Baidu security verification.
3. Open video creation and upload the file once.
4. Wait for the editor, upload completion, and generated/selected cover.
5. Fill the exact user-supplied title. Do not append promotional text automatically.
6. Complete category, description, tags, source, copyright, and declarations shown by the live form.
7. Choose immediate or scheduled publishing and verify date/time.
8. Review, publish once, and verify on content management.

Stop on `百度安全验证`; never automate or bypass it.
